# agy-staff 0.9.3 — Gemini 4 Neon model support

## Highlights

`agy-staff` **0.9.3** adds first-class explicit model support for **Gemini 4 Neon** (`gemini-4-neon-{low,medium,high,xhigh}`) across the companion CLI, skills, DeepSeek Harness plugin, and documentation while keeping all existing built-in defaults (`Gemini 4 Carbon` for `brain`, `Gemini 4 Hydrogen` for workers) and fallback chains unchanged.

### 1. Gemini 4 Neon catalog, alias & compatibility diagnostics (`companion/agy-companion.mjs`)

- **Model registration & alias**: Registered `gemini-4-neon-{low,medium,high,xhigh}` in `KNOWN_MODELS` and `MODEL_FAMILIES` (supporting all 4 effort tiers including `xhigh`), and added the `neon` (`gemini-4-neon`) short alias in `MODEL_ALIASES`.
- **Defaults & fallback preserved**: Built-in defaults (`gemini-4-carbon-medium` for `brain`, `gemini-4-hydrogen-*` for workers) and `BRAIN_FALLBACK_CHAIN` (`Carbon -> Argon -> Hydrogen`) remain unchanged.
- **Compatibility recommendations**: Extended `recommendCompatibleModel` to recognize `gemini-4-neon-*` as a Gemini 4 tier so older `agy` catalogs or accounts without Neon receive same-effort (or `xhigh -> high`) Gemini 4 recommendations without silent fallback.
- **Usage text fix**: Updated companion CLI usage help to include `xh` in `--effort <l|m|h|xh>`.

### 2. Skills & DeepSeek Harness (`skills/`, `pi-skills/`, `opencode-skills/`, `dsh/`, `assets/`)

- **Canonical & generated skills**: Added the `neon` alias to `skills/{brain,ask,staffer,researcher,reviewer,implementer}/SKILL.md` and regenerated `pi-skills/` and `opencode-skills/` with zero drift.
- **DeepSeek Harness (`dsh/index.mjs`, `dsh/client.js`, `dsh/package.json`)**:
  - Updated `agy_brain` and `agy_worker` tool `model` parameter documentation to include `"neon"`.
  - Fixed `shortModelTag` to recognize `"neon"` and render `Neon` (instead of falling back to `Carbon` / `Hydrogen` in sidebar cards and multi-turn pills).
  - Added elemental `PixelIconNeon` ($Z=10$ noble-gas glow ring) in `dsh/client.js` (rendered for Neon brain threads) and standalone `assets/icons/neon.svg`.

### 3. Reference docs, manifests & tests

- Synced `docs/REFERENCE.md` and `docs/REFERENCE.zh-CN.md` with `neon` alias and `gemini-4-neon` `xhigh` support.
- Bumped manifests (`package.json`, `dsh/package.json`, `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `.codex-plugin/plugin.json`) to `0.9.3` and added `"neon"` to plugin keywords/tags.
- Added offline test coverage in `tests/fake-agy.mjs`, `tests/modes.test.mjs`, `tests/brain.test.mjs`, `tests/triage.test.mjs`, and `tests/dsh-plugin.test.mjs`.
